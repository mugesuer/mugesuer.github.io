About

Paths & Passions

Originally from Istanbul, I relocated to Berlin in 2016 to pursue a Master's degree in Economics at Humboldt University — marking the start of my academic journey. I went on to complete my Ph.D. at the Berlin School of Economics (BSoE), where my research focus sharpened around behavioral questions of gender and identity.

I am now a postdoctoral researcher at the Halle Institute for Economic Research (IWH), deepening my work in experimental design, behavioral economics, and the use of AI in experimental research — with a strong policy-relevant orientation.

Beyond the lab, I'm drawn to Jung, documentary filmmaking, and long walks with my dog Yuki. A curious and proud nerd, I'm fascinated by the psychological underpinnings of social structures.

Research

Working Papers & Projects

2024
Working Paper
Gender Identity and Economic Decision-Making Under Uncertainty
Examines how internalized gender norms shape individual risk preferences and strategic behavior in incentivized experimental settings, with implications for understanding persistent gender gaps in labor and financial markets.
2023
Working Paper
Stereotyping and Statistical Discrimination: Evidence from a Controlled Laboratory Study
Uses a novel experimental design to disentangle taste-based discrimination from statistical discrimination, testing how employer beliefs about group-level productivity shape individual hiring decisions.
2023
In Progress
Using AI as a Research Tool in Experimental Economics
Explores methodological applications of large language models in the design and analysis of economic experiments, evaluating validity, scalability, and the limits of AI-generated participant responses.
2022
Working Paper
Social Dilemmas and Gender: Cooperation, Identity, and the Threat of Exclusion
Investigates how gender identity moderates cooperative behavior in social dilemma games, focusing on the role of exclusion threats and group composition in shaping equilibrium outcomes.
